Lots and Homes Over Time

How the size of Beverly's single-family lots and homes has changed by decade built, and how assessed values vary with lot size.

Overview

Beverly has 8,580 single-family properties. This page groups them by the decade the house was built, using City assessor records, and compares how large the homes and lots are in each group. It then shows how assessed values vary with lot size.

The figures describe the homes standing today, so they include any additions made since a house was built. See "Methodology & Limitations" below.

Homes by Decade Built

The median home built in the 1950s has 1,474 square feet of finished living area. The median home built in the 2000s has 2,982.

Assessed Value by Lot Size

The median assessed value of a single-family home on a lot under 5,000 square feet is $601,500. On a lot of 20,000 square feet or more, it is $1,002,700.

What assessed value means

Assessed value is the City Assessor's estimate of a property's value for property tax purposes, here for FY2026. It is not a sale price or an appraisal. Homes on larger lots are also larger homes, so the differences above reflect land and building together.

Methodology & Limitations

How these figures were made

  • Homes: every parcel the Assessor codes as a single-family dwelling (land use code 101) in the City of Beverly GIS parcel layer. Condominiums, two-family, and multi-family properties are not included.
  • Lot size: the Assessor's recorded land area in acres, converted to square feet.
  • Home size: finished living area as recorded by the Assessor. Gross building area, which includes unfinished space such as basements and garages, is not used.
  • Decade built: the Assessor's recorded year built. Homes built before 1900 are grouped together. 4 homes with no recorded year are excluded from the decade tables.
  • Medians: the middle value. Averages are shown beside medians for home size only, because a few very large houses raise averages, most visibly for homes built before 1900.
  • Adjusted comparison: a regression of the logarithm of finished living area on decade built, the logarithm of lot size, and zoning district (the 8 districts with at least 50 single-family homes), with robust standard errors, fit to 8,576 homes. Open Beverly's own calculation; it describes association, not cause.
  • Renovations: finished area is the size of the home today. A house that has had an addition appears larger than it was when built, so older homes may show more finished area than they originally had.
  • Replaced homes: a home that replaced a demolished one appears under its new year built. The data does not show how many homes were demolished.
  • Small groups: the 2020s include only 2020 through 2026. Groups with fewer than 30 homes are not reported.
  • Data: the City's records were accessed on October 22, 2026. The Assessor's figures are not verified here.
